### Case Study 1 — Discord Hackathon Bot
**Secteur** : Developer Community / Students
**Problème** : Finding relevant hackathons is time-consuming — events are scattered across dozens of platforms and most people miss them.
**Solution** : Built a Discord bot that automatically scrapes and aggregates hackathon listings, filters by relevance, and posts them directly in a dedicated server for a community to discover.
**Résultat** : Community members get curated hackathon opportunities without any manual search. The server became a go-to resource for student developers.
**Stack** : Python, Discord API, web scraping

---

### Case Study 2 — Automated Internship Finder
**Secteur** : Students / Job Seekers
**Problème** : Monitoring internship listings across multiple platforms daily is repetitive and easy to miss.
**Solution** : Built an automation that continuously monitors job boards for internship offers matching specific criteria and sends alerts automatically.
**Résultat** : *(En cours de construction)* — removes the need to manually check listings every day, ensuring no opportunity is missed.
**Stack** : Python, automation workflows

---

### Case Study 3 — Cold Outbound Email Automation
**Secteur** : Sales / Business Development
**Problème** : Manual prospecting via email and Google Maps is slow, inconsistent, and doesn't scale.
**Solution** : Built a full cold outbound system that scrapes leads from Google Maps, enriches contact data, and sends personalized email sequences automatically.
**Résultat** : Fully automated prospecting pipeline — from lead discovery to first contact — running without manual intervention.
**Stack** : Python, Google Maps API, email automation
